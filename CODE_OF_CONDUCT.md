# Code of conduct

This project is a personal engineering portfolio. It is open to questions, bug reports,
suggestions and review comments, and everyone taking part is expected to keep the space
respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and assume good faith. Criticize code, designs and ideas, never people.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable: say what you saw, what you expected and why.
- Accept that this is a one-maintainer project; replies and merges can take time.
- Respect other people's privacy. Never post personal data, credentials, invitation
  codes, private recordings or transcripts in issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, sustained disruption or repeated unwanted contact.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or attempts to misuse the hosted demo.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to
any other space where someone is clearly representing the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block accounts
that break this code. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/savbiz). Report security vulnerabilities through
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead, so that they stay private until fixed. Reports about
conduct are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without consent.
